| [Split Expenses Calculator](https://calculatorai.app/calculators/group_social/split-expenses-calculator) | `split-expenses-calculator` |
<!-- gen:calculators end -->

**Split Expenses works differently from every other calculator.** Dividing rent and bills is the one question you cannot answer alone, so instead of a share button it produces a **shareable link**: enter the shared costs, choose even or income-proportional splitting, and press *Create shareable link*. Everyone who opens it sees the same numbers and can add their own income or a missing expense — **no account on either side**. Changes save for everyone holding the link, which expires 90 days after the last edit. Alongside what each person owes, it shows what each person has **left over**, which is usually the number that settles the argument.
