## Shared across all ten accounting documents

They share one editor and one PDF pipeline, so every feature below works everywhere:

- **Fill with AI** — type a sentence (*"Send Globex Corporation $4,200 for May consulting hours, Net 30, my standard 20% tax"*) and the form, client, line items, dates and taxes fill in.
- **PDF / image import** — drop a screenshot, photo or PDF of any billing document and the AI reads it into the template: sender, customer, items, taxes, notes, banking details.
- **CSV import with AI mapping** — bulk-import historical invoices from any spreadsheet; the AI maps your columns to ours, no template required.
- **Save to your account** — revisit, edit, duplicate, archive. Deleted documents go to a Trash bin, not the void.
- **Email straight to your client** — send the PDF in-app with an editable sender name, reply-to and message body. You and the client get the same branded email.
- **Reusable address book** — clients you've billed before are one tap away next time.
- **Multi-currency** — display, send and store in 30+ currencies, formatted to each locale's conventions.
- **Translated into all 10 site languages.**

## Dynamic QR codes

The generator makes both kinds. A **static** code encodes the destination directly — one-and-done, works forever, nothing to track. A **dynamic** code encodes a short link you own, which means after the poster is printed you can still **change where it points** and **see who scanned it** (30-day trend, country, device, browser) in the [QR Codes Tracker](trackers.md#qr-codes-tracker).

Free includes one active dynamic code so you can run the full loop; the generator itself — static codes, colours, shapes, logo, PNG/SVG export — is free and needs no account.
