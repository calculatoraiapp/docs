# Pricing & limits

Live pricing page: **<https://calculatorai.app/pricing>**

| Tier | What it is |
|---|---|
| **Anonymous** | Every calculator, every document tool, trackers in demo mode, every methodology page. No account needed. |
| **Free, signed in** | Keeps you forever. Daily caps on PDFs, saves and AI requests, plus a few lifetime caps. Export is never capped. |
| **Trial** | **30 days of full Pro on signup — no credit card, no auto-charge.** |
| **Pro** | **$14.99/month** or **$119/year**. Removes every cap. |

**When the trial ends** your account drops to Free. Everything you created during it stays — visible, exportable, never deleted. Only *new-creation* buttons gate.

---

## The rules behind the pricing

These are stated publicly at [calculatorai.app/promises](https://calculatorai.app/promises) because they're enforced in the codebase, not by intention:

1. **No watermark** on anything you generate — invoice, quote, receipt, QR code or PDF report, on any plan.
2. **CSV export of your own data is free on every tier**, with no row caps. Your numbers are never held hostage.
3. **No card to start.** The 30-day Pro trial takes no payment details and cannot auto-charge.
4. **Data stays visible after Pro lapses.** Expiry limits creating new items; it never deletes what exists.
5. **Delete means a bin, not the void** — removed records sit in a restorable Trash for 30 days.

**The math is free forever.** Core compute, formula explanations, breakdowns and results are never paywalled. Pro removes caps on AI, saves and history; it never gates the arithmetic.

## Embedding is outside all of this

Putting a calculator on **your own website** is free for any site, commercial ones included, with no account, no API key and no cap on how many you embed. See the [embed guide](embed-widget.md).

## Billing mechanics

Payments run through **[Stripe](https://stripe.com/docs/security)** — CalculatorAI never sees or stores a card number. You get a welcome email when you subscribe, a confirmation showing exactly when access ends when you cancel, and yearly subscribers get a heads-up about a week before renewal. Cancel from your account page in one click.
